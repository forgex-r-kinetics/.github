## ForgeX lets teams change business rules without coding

[ForgeX](https://forgex.r-kinetics.com/) is a no-code logic engine that helps teams create, test, and update business rules without relying on developers for every change. Users can describe requirements in plain English, build rules visually, and launch updates in minutes.

### Key features

* No-code visual rule builder
* AI assistant for creating rules from plain English
* Build, test, and launch rules from one place
* Works with existing systems
* Full tracking of rule changes
* Supports pricing, discounts, approvals, and other business logic

### Why it matters

Small business changes can often get stuck in development queues. ForgeX gives teams more control over routine rule updates, helping them respond faster without changing the systems they already use.
